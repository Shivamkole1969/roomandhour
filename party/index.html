Who Took the Christmas Star?

A mystery party game on one phone. Pass it round, read your secret card, and work out which of you stole the star. Nothing to print, no prep, no sign-up.

  • 4–8 players
  • About 30 minutes
  • Ages 10+
  • Free

You have a game in progress. Carry on

How it works

  1. Type everyone's name. The phone gives each of you a character at Holly House.
  2. Pass the phone round. Each player secretly reads their card. One of you is the thief, and only the thief knows it.
  3. Three short rounds: say where you were at 10:30, look at the evidence, question the suspects.
  4. Everyone votes in secret. Then the phone reveals the truth, room by room.

Every game deals a new thief, new alibis and new clues, so you can play again straight away.

Questions

Do we need to print anything?

No. One phone does everything. Keep it on the table and pass it when it asks you to.

Is it OK for children?

Yes. Nobody gets hurt: a precious star goes missing. Ages 10 and up can play; younger children can team up with an adult.

Can the thief really be caught?

Always. Every innocent player's clues fit together, and every game is checked by a computer to make sure the thief's story can be broken. The thief wins only if the group is fooled.

Does it save our game?

On this phone only, so you can pause it. Nothing is sent anywhere and there's no account.
