Style

Short words with big letters are easiest to read. Everything happens in your browser: your word is scrambled into the link, and nothing is stored on a server.

How to see it

  1. Hold the picture level, about an arm's length away.
  2. Put a finger halfway between your eyes and the screen, and look at the fingertip.
  3. Behind your finger, the two dots on top turn into three. Keep your eyes there, and the middle picture sharpens into the hidden word.
  • Can't get it? Move your finger a little closer or farther, or move the phone.
  • If your eyes feel strained, stop and blink. It gets easier with practice.

Why it works

This is a random-dot stereogram, an idea from vision scientist Béla Julesz at Bell Labs around 1960. Each panel on its own is pure noise. In the second panel, the dots that form your word are moved a few dots to the side. When each eye looks at a different panel, your brain matches the dots, and the shifted patch appears at a different depth: your word. Read more.
